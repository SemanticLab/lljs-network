    About the data

    Who appears here

    Interviewees
    People identified as interviewees in the project’s 27 processed transcripts. An interviewee who also played jazz has one node.
    Jazz shrines
    Brooklyn places classified as shrines in the project atlas. A shrine can have no recorded connections among the people shown here.
    Musicians
    Other people with a direct shrine relationship and evidence of musical activity from Wikidata or the project. Each musician’s record gives the basis for inclusion.

    Reading the graph

    Scroll over the graph to zoom in on names, or drag to pan. Select a node to follow its connections. Select an edge to read its cited transcript passages and open the original block records. In Focus view, edges show relationship names and arrowheads follow the recorded direction. A number beside a label counts additional relationships on that line.
